Development of Synthetic Clots with Shear-Induced Fragmentation for Improved In Vitro Neurointerventional Device Testing
Overview
Ischemic stroke is a leading cause of death and long-term disability, with mechanical thrombectomy now an established standard of care. A need exists for validated synthetic clot analogs for in vitro thrombectomy testing and device comparison. While existing synthetic clot analogs accurately reproduce the compressive and shear mechanical properties of human thrombus, they generally fail to replicate clinically relevant clot fragmentation and distal embolization observed during thrombectomy. We present a synthetic thrombus with tunable, physiologically accurate fragmentation behavior and quantified particulation
